# Classic Apps

<!-- Jira: PRODUCT-15500 -->

**Gen3 replacement**: Native Apps

**Phase 2 required**: no

## What changes

Classic apps will be removed in Phase 3. Customers need to ensure that usage of classic apps is low or accepted to be removed, and understand the classic app replacements.

## Customer actions

- Review classic app usage in the tenant
- Identify native app replacements for each classic app in use
- Transition users to native apps
