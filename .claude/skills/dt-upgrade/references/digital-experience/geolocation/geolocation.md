# Geolocation

<!-- Jira: PRODUCT-16593 -->

**Gen3 replacement**: Complete geolocation enrichment in RUM

**Phase 2 required**: no

## What changes

Capturing and enrichment of geolocation data (continent, country, region, city) is available out of the box for both web and mobile technologies. Exploring data by geolocation attributes is accessible in the Experience Vitals, Users & Sessions, and Error Inspector apps, as well as via dashboards or notebooks.
